eBay — A-Z of Authenticity

eBay · Authenticity Guarantee ✦

NotReal directed eBay's "A to Z of Authenticity" — an alphabet-driven film celebrating the platform's authentication promise. We joined the team for 3D animation and CG simulations, helping bring the live-action plates and product transitions to life across a frame-by-frame visual journey.

Play Main Film

Overview

We contributed to the 3D animation and CG simulations on a film built from the ground up around the alphabet — twenty-six product moments, a relentless rhythm, and live-action plates threaded together by tactile CG transitions. Each letter became its own micro-set, with hero objects rebuilt in 3D so reflections, surfaces and motion could match the precision of the campaign idea.

The result is a fast, joyful piece of brand storytelling that earns eBay's authenticity claim through detail rather than declaration.
