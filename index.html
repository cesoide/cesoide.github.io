+ CESAR · SP · 2026

Atmosphere, restraint, and materials you can feel.

I'm Cesar Barbosa, a 3D designer and animator based in São Paulo, working remotely with studios and brands worldwide.

My work sits where animation meets design direction — I care about how a surface catches light and how a move feels as much as the technique behind it. That's the sensibility I bring whether the brief is a product film, a brand campaign, or a single frame.

Over the past years I've collaborated with studios like Buck, Oddfellows and FutureDeluxe, contributing to work for Microsoft, Meta, Verizon and others.

Currently available for remote projects, ideally from concept through final delivery.

Get in touch at cesar@cesoi.de — or follow personal work on Instagram.
