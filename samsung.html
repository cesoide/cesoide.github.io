Samsung — Cheil Screensavers

Samsung · Galaxy S Lockscreens ✦

FutureDeluxe partnered with Samsung's Cheil agency to develop a screensaver system for the Galaxy S series — abstract, hero-grade lockscreen visuals built to define the device's identity. We contributed motion design across a library of looping, frame-perfect wallpapers exploring light, motion and material in 1:1.

Play R&D Reel

Overview

We crafted a series of square lockscreen visuals exploring light, ribbon, fluid and crystalline motifs — each built to read clearly on the Galaxy S display while sustaining a calm, premium presence over time. Every wallpaper was authored as a frame-perfect loop, optimized for low-power always-on rendering.
