Verizon — Investor Day 2022

Verizon · Investor Day 2022 ✦

Hornet partnered with Verizon to deliver the visual language for the 2022 Investor Day — a fast, type-driven film that translates the company's network promise into kinetic CG narrative. Director Eric Lane led the team; we contributed 3D design and animation across hero product moments, environments and infographic sequences.

Play Main Film

Overview

The film weaves typography, motion design and CG into a single editorial language built for the investor stage. Every product hero, environment and data moment was rebuilt in 3D so the camera could move freely through scale and detail, while motion design carried the narrative beat-by-beat.

Output included the full Investor Day master and a system of reusable motion modules that Verizon could redeploy across launch surfaces.
