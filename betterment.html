Betterment · Cash Reserve

Betterment · Cash Reserve *

State Design partnered with Betterment to bring the Cash Reserve campaign to life · a series of character-led, design-driven films explaining how the platform's high-yield savings tool works. Working in Cinema 4D, Redshift and After Effects, I contributed 3D animation across product moments and motion design beats.

Play Main Film

Overview

I worked alongside the State Design team on 3D animation across Cash Reserve's product hero moments · translating financial mechanics into a tactile, character-led visual language that feels approachable and confident.

The deliverables included a master film and a system of motion modules used across digital and social surfaces.
