Singleton — Discover True Richness

The Singleton · Discover True Richness ✦

Stink directed The Singleton's "Discover True Richness" — a cinematic film exploring the brand's playful, sensorial side through abstract worlds and tactile light. Joining the HardcoreFX team as motion designer, we contributed CG simulations and product moments to the campaign's visual language.

Play Main Film

Overview

Working at HardcoreFX under director Pedro Tejada, we crafted simulations and CG hero moments that translate The Singleton's "true richness" into liquid, light and abstract environments. Every shot was rebuilt in 3D so the camera could move freely through the brand's tactile world.

Output included the master film, a system of looping motion stills and a library of process frames documenting the lookdev journey.
